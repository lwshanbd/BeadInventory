### Suggestions (&lt; 80)

- **@Observable subscription (the concern you raised) — real but low-risk, and mirrors the shipped `dynamicBg` pattern.** Confidence 65. `Palette.n50…n900` now resolve through `dynamicNeutral()`, whose observable read of `resolvedLight/Dark.bg` happens *inside* the `UIColor { trait in … }` closure — i.e. when UIKit resolves the color, **not** during SwiftUI `body` evaluation. So `withObservationTracking` never sees the read and no dependency on the theme's `bg` is registered. System light↔dark still works (trait-driven). Live *preset/swatch* changes work only because the view driving them (`ColorModeView`) independently observes `activeSchemeID`/`resolvedLight` and re-renders wholesale, rebuilding the tokens. The latent gap: a view that references *only* neutral tokens (text/border/subtle-surface) and doesn't otherwise observe `ThemeManager`, while visible during a theme edit, could show stale neutrals until the next rebuild. Note the comment at `ThemeManager.swift:86-90` asserts the subscription *is* established during body eval — that's inaccurate for this dynamic-provider pattern and worth correcting so future readers don't rely on it.
